Ratan Tata

1937 - 2024 | A Visionary Leader, A True Gem of India

About

Ratan Naval Tata was an Indian industrialist and former chairman of Tata Sons. He was known for his vision, humility and his great contribution to India. He led the Tata Group to great heights and will always be remembered for his kindness and his inspiring words for the young generation.

Timeline

  • 1937: Born in Mumbai
  • 1962: Joined Tata Group
  • 1991: Became Chairman of Tata Sons
  • 2008: Launched Tata Nano
  • 2024: An era ended, but legacy lives on
